SUMMERIS

A summer collection designed around breathable fabrics, relaxed silhouettes, and a neutral palette created for effortless everyday dressing.

(INFO)

Project Plain Set Out To Prove That Simplicity Isn't Easy. Without Prints, Graphics, Or Excessive Detailing, The Challenge Became Creating A Collection With Character Through Thoughtful Design, Considered Fits, And Material Selection.

The Result Is A Wardrobe Of Understated Essentials Designed To Outlast Seasonal Trends.

These Feel Authentic And Are The Kinds Of Challenges A Minimalist Clothing Brand Would Genuinely Face, Rather Than Making Up Unlikely Production Issues.
