EVERY BRAND IS EXPERIENCED AS ONE.

Founded in Hyderabad,

LabelX grew from years of working across branding, fashion, manufacturing, and digital experiences. We saw ambitious brands navigating disconnected processes, fragmented communication, and teams that rarely shared the same vision. There was a clear opportunity to build something more integrated.

Today, LabelX operates as a multidisciplinary studio where strategy, design, product development, manufacturing, retail experiences, and campaigns come together with intention. We collaborate with fashion and lifestyle brands to create work that is considered, commercially relevant, and built to endure.
